Be one of our first couples

Before Ourlight can go public on Google Play, Google needs people to test it for 14 days. We're looking for 10 couples.

What it takes

  • Two Android phones, one each, with Android 8 or newer
  • A Google account each (the one you use on the Play Store)
  • A few seconds a day for 14 days: tap your weather, see your partner's
  • One honest line of feedback at the end

How to join

  1. Email us both of your Google account emails, using the button below.
  2. We add you to the test and send you a link.
  3. Open the link on your phone, tap "Become a tester", and install Ourlight from the Play Store.
  4. Sign in, invite your partner from the app, and you're set.

You can join on your own and invite your partner later, as long as they can join within a few days.

Email us to join

Or write to hello@ourlight.app with "Testing Ourlight" in the subject. We only use your email for the test. See the privacy policy.
