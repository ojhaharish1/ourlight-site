4. Pairing with one partner

Ourlight works between two people. You pair with exactly one partner, by inviting their email address or by sharing a 6-character code. Pairing is a shared decision: only invite someone who has agreed to use Ourlight with you.

Once paired, each of you can see:

  • the other's first name, their daily weather, and any feeling word or short line they chose to share;
  • the care messages, verses and "about us" notes the other sends;
  • the other's care profile answers, which is what the care ideas are built from;
  • whether the other has paused Ourlight, shown only as "taking a break";
  • the joint weekly score, built from the guesses you both made.

Check-ins marked "just for me" never leave the sender's phone, and neither partner can see the other's.

Either of you can unpair at any time from Settings. The other person is not told why. Shared history already on their phone stays on their phone.
