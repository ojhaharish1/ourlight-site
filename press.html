About Ourlight

Ourlight is a couples app for the first five minutes after a hard day. Each partner taps their inner weather once a day, from sunny to storm, low battery or want closeness. The other sees it at a time they chose, with ways to care drawn only from their partner's own answers about what helps them. There are no streaks, no gender-based advice and no predictions, and the app has no ads or tracking.
